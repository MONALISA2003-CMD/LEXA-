# LEXA Emerald Fusion — Quick Brand Guide

## Palette

- **Emerald:** `#10B981` — primary brand action and selected navigation.
- **Teal:** `#13B8B0` — supporting brand tone and secondary chart series.
- **Electric blue:** `#496CFF` — interactive and analytical accent.
- **Sunshine:** `#FFC857` — selective highlights and progress cues.
- **Deep navy:** `#13233F` — headings, wordmark and dark surfaces.
- **Cool background:** `#F5F8FC` — application canvas.

## Logo concept

A clear check/forward mark inside a rounded gradient tile, paired with a strongly set LEXA wordmark. The mark should remain simple at small sizes; use SVG wherever possible.

## Usage

- Use the full dark wordmark on a white or pale background.
- Use the light wordmark on dark backgrounds.
- Use the icon mark alone for favicon, compact navigation and app icons.
- Emerald and teal communicate brand identity; electric blue supports charts and interaction; sunshine is for selective emphasis. Do not colour every control differently.
- Keep text on light or dark solid surfaces with strong contrast. Do not place small financial figures directly over gradients.
- Preserve the logo's proportions; do not stretch, rotate or add extra outlines/shadows.

## Voice

Write for owners, cashiers, stock managers and bookkeepers—not developers. Prefer “Stock history” over “inventory ledger”, “Product code” over “SKU”, “Money recorded” over “payment channel event”, and short recovery instructions over database or API error details.
